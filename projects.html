Sankofa

Feature Film Screenplay • Historical Drama (119 Pages)

Set in 1539 AD during the Oyo Empire, Sankofa is an epic historical drama that weaves a powerful narrative around figures such as Alaafin Ofinran, Orompotoniyun, and Adesuwa. Utilizing authentic cultural concepts like Bàbá, Ó dá, and Moremi mi, the script grounds an epic scale in deep, character-driven truth.
